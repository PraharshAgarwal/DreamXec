Database Optimization & Indexing Layout

Compounded Indexing for Tenant Query Routing

Every dashboard query searches by organization first. We define index partitions globally:

@@index([organizationId, status])
@@index([labId, status])
@@index([projectId, createdAt])

Tenant Partitioning via Sharding Key

For high-throughput multi-org scales, MongoDB shards the database using organizationId as the shard key. This directs specific university/corporate traffic to its respective shard.

IP Immutability hashing

Lab notebooks are logged with a SHA-256 hashSignature (combining author, notes, and timestamp) providing cryptographic validation for licensing audits.

Stage-Gate Approvals

Pre-Verification Gate System

Before a research project goes live for public matching, funding releases, or university equipment booking, it must clear the following **4 mandatory checkpoints**:

1

Gate 1: Academic Validation (Idea Review)

Student ideas must be scored and approved by a department Professor or Incubator Manager. Ideas are evaluated based on **Novelty**, **Feasibility**, and **Impact**. Once approved, they convert to a project in the Discovery stage.

2

Gate 2: Compliance Validation (IRB Protocol Approval)

Projects involving human/animal subjects, hazardous chemicals, or restricted datasets submit an IRB protocol. A Compliance Officer must approve the protocol, locking the ethical framework of the project.

3

Gate 3: Resource Clearance (Lab & Equipment check)

The Principal Investigator (PI) verifies scheduling parameters and hourly booking costs for shared infrastructure resources. This prevents conflict booking of major lab hardware.

4

Gate 4: Budget & Financial Clearance (Grant Matching)

The Project's milestones are mapped directly to a funding agency's grant rules. Corporate sponsors allocate funds to specific stage outcomes (e.g. 30% budget released upon Prototype sign-off).
