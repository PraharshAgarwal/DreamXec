# DreamXec Theme and Typography Rules

When generating or updating UI components, styles, or the general theme for the DreamXec application, always adhere to the following design guidelines to ensure consistency.

## 1. Primary Colors

The primary color palette is bold, energetic, and expressive. It is designed to reflect curiosity, imagination, and the spirit of innovation.

*   **Orange:** `#FE4E1A` (RGB: 254, 78, 26)
    *   *Meaning:* Represents creativity, enthusiasm, and imagination = new ideas. It reflects the spark of curiosity that drives research and exploration.
*   **Forest Green:** `#0F534A` (RGB: 15, 83, 74)
    *   *Meaning:* Symbolizes growth, knowledge, and progress. It represents the long-term impact of research on society and development.
*   **Bright Red:** `#F4002C` (RGB: 244, 0, 44)
    *   *Meaning:* Represents energy, passion, and determination, reflecting the drive to question, experiment, and push boundaries.
*   **Deep Purple:** `#4717AE` (RGB: 71, 23, 174)
    *   *Meaning:* Symbolizes wisdom, intelligence, and imagination, connecting to deeper thinking and intellectual exploration.

## 2. Secondary Colors

The secondary color palette adds vibrancy and flexibility to the brand. These colors support the primary palette and create visually engaging communication.

*   **Bright Yellow:** `#FFDD33` (RGB: 255, 221, 51)
*   **Bright Green:** `#9EFC5F` (RGB: 158, 252, 95)
*   **Pastel Pink:** `#FEB3FC` (RGB: 254, 179, 252)
*   **Vibrant Blue:** `#18D7E8` (RGB: 24, 215, 232)

## 3. Typography

### Primary Typeface: Poppins
Poppins is the primary typeface due to its clean, modern, and highly readable design. The geometric structure reflects clarity, precision, and structured thinking.

### Secondary Typeface: Badlooking Brush
Badlooking Brush is the secondary typeface. It adds personality and expression. Its handwritten brush style brings a human and creative touch, representing curiosity, experimentation, and the free flow of ideas. Use selectively for highlights, annotations, or emphasis.

## 4. Type Hierarchy

The type hierarchy is designed to create a clear and structured flow of information.

*   **Headline:** `Poppins Black` (Used for main titles and key messages to grab attention)
*   **Subline:** `Poppins Medium` (Used to introduce sections and provide supporting context)
*   **Bodycopy:** `Poppins Regular` (Used for detailed content and paragraphs to ensure comfortable readability)
*   **Annotation:** `Badlooking Brush` (Used for small notes, captions, or supporting information that adds clarity without distracting from the main message, or for stylized highlights)
